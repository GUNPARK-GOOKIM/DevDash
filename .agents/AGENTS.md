# Workspace Rules & Social Media Guidelines

## 📱 Social Media Posting Rules (LinkedIn, Threads, X)
1. **No Comment Replies:** Never reply to any comments on any post (including user's own posts).
2. **Strict Accuracy (No Fake Information):** Never make fake claims or state projects are open-sourced/released when they are still in active development (e.g. DevDash is still being built).
3. **Research First:** Always research topics and technical concepts thoroughly before drafting posts.
4. **Optimal Post Length:** Keep posts balanced — not too long, not too short. Avoid AI-sounding text; keep it punchy, human, and conversational (2-4 concise sentences or bullet points).
